SAMPLE CONTRACT (fair clauses from clause_library/fair_clauses.json).



1. Fair Supplier Payment Terms
Buyer shall pay Supplier the invoiced amount within thirty (30) days of receipt of Goods Receipt Note (GRN), inspection acceptance, and a valid tax invoice, whichever is later. Supplier shall submit invoices with GSTIN, HSN code, PO reference, and supporting documents as specified in the Purchase Order. Retention money of five percent (5%) of each invoice shall be withheld until successful completion of all delivery obligations and expiry of the defect liability period. Retention shall be released within thirty (30) days of DLP completion certificate issuance. Late payments beyond the thirty-day credit period shall accrue simple interest at the rate of one percent (1%) per month on the outstanding amount.

2. Fair Supplier Delivery Terms
Supplier shall deliver all goods Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) to Buyer's designated site as per Incoterms 2020. Risk of loss or damage shall transfer to Buyer only upon delivery and acceptance at the designated site. Delivery shall be in accordance with the delivery schedule set forth in the Purchase Order. Supplier shall notify Buyer of dispatch at least seven (7) days in advance. In the event of delay attributable to Supplier, liquidated damages at the rate of zero point five percent (0.5%) of the delayed item value per week of delay shall apply, capped at ten percent (10%) of the total Purchase Order value. Buyer may cancel the order if delay exceeds eight (8) weeks without prejudice to other remedies.

3. Fair Supplier Pricing Clause
All prices set forth in the Purchase Order are firm and fixed for a period of twelve (12) months from the Effective Date of this Agreement. Any price revision thereafter shall require mutual written agreement and shall not exceed the change in the Wholesale Price Index (WPI) plus two percent (2%) per annum, calculated on a compounded basis. Supplier shall provide sixty (60) days advance written notice of any proposed price revision along with supporting documentation justifying the increase. Buyer reserves the right to source from alternative suppliers if the revised price exceeds prevailing market rates by more than ten percent (10%). No retroactive price adjustments shall apply.

4. Fair Supplier Quality Standards
All goods supplied shall conform to ISO 9001:2015 quality management standards and any additional specifications referenced in the Purchase Order. Supplier shall conduct Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) at its premises in accordance with an agreed Inspection and Test Plan (ITP) identifying hold points, witness points, and review points. Buyer or its nominated third-party inspection agency shall have the right to witness all hold points. Site Acceptance Testing (SAT) shall be conducted at Buyer's site within fourteen (14) days of delivery. Supplier shall provide material test certificates, calibration certificates, and certificates of conformance for each batch. Goods not conforming to specifications may be rejected within thirty (30) days of delivery.

5. Fair Supplier Warranty Terms
Supplier warrants that all goods supplied shall be free from defects in material and workmanship for a period of twenty-four (24) months from the date of commissioning or twenty-seven (27) months from the date of delivery, whichever occurs first. During the warranty period, Supplier shall, at Buyer's option, repair or replace any defective goods at Supplier's sole cost and expense. Supplier shall respond to warranty claims within forty-eight (48) hours for critical defects affecting operations and within seven (7) calendar days for non-critical defects. If Supplier fails to remedy a critical defect within fourteen (14) days, Buyer may engage a third party at Supplier's cost. The warranty excludes consumable items, normal wear and tear, and damage caused by Buyer's misuse.

6. Fair Supplier Defect Liability Period
A Defect Liability Period (DLP) of twelve (12) months shall apply from the date of Site Acceptance Testing completion. During the DLP, Supplier shall rectify any defect attributable to faulty design, materials, or workmanship at its own cost within fourteen (14) calendar days of written notice from Buyer. For emergency defects affecting safety or operations, Supplier shall respond within forty-eight (48) hours and complete rectification within seven (7) days. If Supplier fails to rectify within the specified timeline, Buyer may engage a third party at Supplier's reasonable cost. The retention money of five percent (5%) shall be released within thirty (30) days of issuance of the DLP completion certificate confirming all defects have been satisfactorily rectified.

7. Balanced Supplier Liability Clause
Each party's total aggregate liability to the other under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed one hundred percent (100%) of the total Purchase Order value. Neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages including loss of profit, loss of revenue, or loss of production. The limitations of liability shall not apply to (a) death or personal injury caused by negligence, (b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, (c) gross negligence or willful misconduct, (d) breach of confidentiality obligations, or (e) infringement of third-party intellectual property rights. Each party shall maintain adequate insurance to cover its potential liability under this Agreement.

8. Fair Supplier Indemnity Clause
Each party (the 'Indemnifying Party') shall indemnify and hold harmless the other party (the 'Indemnified Party') against all claims, losses, and reasonable legal costs arising from (a) death or personal injury caused by the Indemnifying Party's negligence, (b) damage to tangible property caused by the Indemnifying Party, and (c) infringement of third-party intellectual property rights by goods or materials provided by the Indemnifying Party. The Indemnified Party shall promptly notify the Indemnifying Party of any claim, allow the Indemnifying Party to conduct the defense, and provide reasonable cooperation. The indemnity shall be limited to the liability cap set forth in the Limitation of Liability clause.

9. Fair Supplier Termination Clause
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing ninety (90) days written notice to the other party. Either party may terminate immediately for material breach that remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice. Upon termination, Supplier shall (a) deliver all completed goods and work-in-progress for which Buyer has paid, (b) return all Buyer-owned materials, tools, and confidential information within fifteen (15) days, and (c) provide reasonable transition assistance for up to thirty (30) days. Buyer shall pay for all goods completed and accepted before termination plus the reasonable cost of materials specifically procured for this Agreement that cannot be mitigated. All outstanding invoices shall be settled within forty-five (45) days of termination.

10. Fair Supplier Force Majeure Clause
Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure to perform due to Force Majeure Events including acts of God, war, terrorism, civil unrest, epidemic, pandemic, cyber attack, fire, flood, earthquake, governmental action, or failure of utilities or telecommunications networks. The affected party shall notify the other within seven (7) days of the Force Majeure Event, describing its impact and expected duration. Both parties shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects. If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than ninety (90) days, either party may terminate this Agreement without liability by providing thirty (30) days written notice. Supplier shall refund any advance payment for goods not delivered due to the Force Majeure Event.
