1. Fair Payment Terms
Client shall pay Consultant the fees set forth in Exhibit A within thirty (30) days of receipt of an accurate and undisputed invoice. In the event of a bona fide dispute regarding any invoice, Client shall pay the undisputed portion when due and the parties shall work in good faith to resolve the disputed portion within fifteen (15) business days. Late payments shall accrue simple interest at the lesser of 1% per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

2. Fair Termination Clause
Either party may terminate this Agreement (a) for material breach by the other party that remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice, or (b) upon sixty (60) days written notice for convenience. Upon termination, Consultant shall be paid for all work completed through the effective date of termination plus any non-cancellable expenses incurred. All provisions that by their nature should survive termination, including confidentiality, limitation of liability, and payment obligations, shall so survive.

3. Balanced Limitation of Liability
Neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages arising out of or related to this Agreement, including lost profits, except in cases of gross negligence, willful misconduct, fraud, or breach of confidentiality obligations. Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the greater of (a) the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement during the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, or (b) one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000). These limitations shall not apply to indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality, or infringement of intellectual property rights.

4. Mutual Confidentiality Clause
Each party (the 'Receiving Party') agrees to hold in strict confidence all non-public information disclosed by the other party (the 'Disclosing Party'), whether in writing, orally, or through any other medium, that is clearly identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential (collectively, 'Confidential Information'). The Receiving Party shall use at least the same degree of care as it uses with its own similar confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care. Confidential Information excludes information that (a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach, (b) was lawfully known prior to disclosure, (c) is independently developed without use of Confidential Information, or (d) is required to be disclosed by law, provided the Receiving Party gives prompt notice and reasonable cooperation to limit such disclosure.

5. Balanced IP Ownership
Consultant retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing materials, tools, methodologies, and know-how ('Consultant IP'). Upon full payment, Consultant assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in the deliverables specifically created for Client under this Agreement ('Work Product'), excluding Consultant IP. Consultant grants Client a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide license to use any Consultant IP incorporated into the Work Product solely to the extent necessary for Client to use the Work Product. Each party represents that its contributions will not infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party.

6. Fair Employee Notice Period
The Employee shall provide thirty (30) days written notice to the Employer prior to resignation. The Employer may, at its sole discretion, waive the notice period in whole or in part or provide pay in lieu of notice calculated at the Employee's base salary rate. During the notice period, the Employee shall continue to perform their duties and cooperate in the orderly transition of responsibilities. The Employer shall not withhold termination documents, relieving letters, or final settlement on account of the notice period if the Employee has completed at least fifteen (15) days of active service during the notice period.

7. Fair Employer Termination Procedure
The Employer may terminate this Agreement for cause immediately upon written notice in cases of gross misconduct, fraud, willful insubordination, or material breach of employment terms. For performance-related termination, the Employer shall (a) provide a written warning specifying the performance deficiencies, (b) allow the Employee a reasonable improvement period of not less than thirty (30) days, and (c) provide reasonable support and feedback during the improvement period. Termination without cause shall require thirty (30) days written notice or pay in lieu of notice. In all cases, the Employee shall receive a written statement of reasons for termination.

8. Fair Employee Confidentiality Clause
The Employee shall maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary and trade secret information of the Employer for a period of two (2) years following termination of employment. Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Employee, (b) was known to the Employee prior to employment as evidenced by written records, or (c) the Employee is required to disclose by law, court order, or governmental authority. Nothing in this clause shall prevent the Employee from reporting suspected violations of law to any government agency, making protected whistleblower disclosures, or cooperating in any government investigation.

9. Fair Employee Salary Payment Terms
The Employer shall pay the Employee's monthly salary on or before the seventh (7th) day of each calendar month. Salary shall be paid via electronic bank transfer to the Employee's designated bank account. The Employer shall provide the Employee with a detailed written salary statement showing gross salary, all deductions (tax, provident fund, professional tax, insurance), and net salary paid. Any unauthorized deduction from salary is prohibited. In the event of salary payment delay exceeding seven (7) calendar days, simple interest at the rate of 1% per month shall accrue on the outstanding amount from the due date until payment is made. Interest on delayed salary shall be paid along with the next month's salary.

10. Fair Employee Working Hours Clause
The Employee's normal working hours shall not exceed forty-eight (48) hours per week, with a maximum of nine (9) hours per day exclusive of rest intervals. The Employee shall be entitled to a rest break of at least thirty (30) minutes after every five (5) hours of continuous work. Any work performed beyond the normal working hours shall be treated as overtime and compensated at twice (2x) the Employee's regular hourly rate. Overtime shall be voluntary and not mandatory. The Employee is entitled to one (1) full rest day per week, typically Sunday. The total overtime hours in any quarter shall not exceed one hundred twenty-five (125) hours. The Employer shall maintain accurate records of all hours worked and overtime paid.
